AI processing

Your question and text from the scripture are sent to the Google Gemini API for processing to create the reading. Under Google's terms for paid services, this data is not used to train models, but Google may log requests and responses for a limited period to detect policy-violating use. Please do not type identifying or sensitive information (such as ID card numbers or health information) into your question.

Data kept on your device

The record of how many days in a row you have opened the daily hexagram, and the theme you chose, are stored on your device and disappear when you delete the app or delete your account. However, usage events, such as reaching a number of days and changing the theme, are included in the usage data sent to Firebase Analytics. Share cards are created on your device without your question, but the AI summary on the card may reflect what you asked, so please check the preview before sharing. Images leave your device only when you choose to share them to another app. The reading voice uses your device's text-to-speech, which may operate under the policy of the text-to-speech provider you have set.

Service providers we share data with

Google (Firebase, Gemini API, AdMob) · RevenueCat · Apple · Google Play — which may process data outside Thailand, under the safeguards the providers set. We do not sell your personal data.

Retention period

We keep data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account data, fortune journal, reports you submitted and customer data in RevenueCat are deleted immediately. The following data remains after deletion:

  • The hashed device ID and the daily count of free uses, which are no longer linked to an account, kept to prevent deleting an account and creating a new one to get free uses
  • System logs, which expire within about 30 days
  • Data in Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and AdMob, which expires according to Google's retention periods
  • Purchase history that Apple/Google keep as required by law, which is under the care of those providers

Your rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, objection to, restriction of use of, and a copy of your data, and you may withdraw consent at any time. You can delete your account yourself in the app at Settings → Delete account and all data, or see how to delete your account. To exercise your rights, contact ahts.13.14.07@gmail.com. You may also file a complaint with the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee.
