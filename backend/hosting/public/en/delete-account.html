Delete in the app (immediately)

  1. Open the I Ching AI app
  2. Tap the ⚙︎ Settings icon in the top right corner of the home page
  3. Scroll down to "Account", tap Delete account and all data, and confirm

Your account, fortune journal, conversations, "Did it come true?" notes, remaining coins and the reports you submitted are deleted from the server immediately, along with the data on your device.

Data that remains after deletion

  • The hashed device ID and the daily count of free uses, which are no longer linked to an account, kept to prevent deleting an account and creating a new one to get free uses
  • System logs, which expire within about 30 days
  • Data in Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and AdMob, which expires according to Google's retention periods
  • Purchase history that Apple/Google keep as required by law

Delete without opening the app

Send an email to ahts.13.14.07@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account", along with the account ID shown in Settings → Account (or the Apple/Google email linked to it). We will act on it within 30 days.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription automatically. Please also cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.
