## Results

- English reply: 17/17 readings contain no Thai script outside the three card labels; 3/3 safety replies are English. **20/20**.
- Cards: 17/17 readings contain `[ดวงเด่น]`, `[ควรระวัง]`, `[คำแนะนำ]` once each, in order, after the opening paragraph. **17/17**. Opening paragraphs are 118-159 words in total including cards (spec asked 40-70 for the opening paragraph; length was not checked per paragraph).
- Hexagram citation: no reply named a wrong hexagram. The literal English name of the primary or relating hexagram appears in 13/17 (the primary's own name in 10/17; #1, #3 and #9 name only the relating hexagram and describe the primary by image, e.g. "The Creative hexagram" for Heaven). The other 4 (#7, #14, #16, #17) refer to it only by image or alternative name (e.g. "the young bride" for Secondary Bride, "the hexagram of the Family" for Household). Other hexagram-like words in the replies (Thunder, Mountain, Abundance, Gathering, Retreat) are ordinary words or trigram images. Judged by hand from a keyword scan, all 17 are consistent with the cast hexagram; this is a human reading, not an exact-match test.
- Crisis: 3/3 went to the safety path (no Gemini call), English message, mentions local emergency services and findahelpline.com. **3/3**.
- Share summary (`done.shareSummary`) was English, one sentence, in all 17.

Verdict: PASS (20/20 English, 17/17 cards, 3/3 crisis). Caveats: a 20-question sample of my own wording, not the original set.
