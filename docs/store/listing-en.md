I Ching AI brings the I Ching, the Book of Changes, to your phone. It is a Chinese oracle more than three thousand years old. Hold a question in mind, toss three coins six times in the traditional way, and receive a clear, easy-to-read reading from an AI hermit that always draws on the original text.

You can also draw Kau Cim fortune sticks (Chinese fortune sticks) from two classic oracle sets.

☯ Traditional I Ching reading
• Toss three coins six times to cast any of the 64 hexagrams and 384 lines
• Shake your phone to toss the coins, with sound and a scroll-style look
• See your primary hexagram, changing lines and resulting hexagram, with the judgment from the original text

🔮 Clear readings from AI
• The AI hermit interprets your hexagram for your own question: work, money, love, peace of mind, or a big decision
• Every reading is rooted in the I Ching text, not made up from nothing
• You can always read the plain scripture text, even without an AI reading

🎋 Kau Cim fortune sticks
• Draw from the Guanyin Oracle (100 slips) or the Sixty Jiazi Oracle (60 slips)
• Shake the fortune stick cylinder, confirm with the moon blocks, then read the verse with its translation
• Tap "Interpret" and the AI hermit relates the slip to your question

📅 Daily hexagram and New Year reading
• A daily hexagram with a short thought to start your day
• Add the daily hexagram widget to your home screen
• Turn on a daily hexagram notification if you like
• Cast a reading for the year ahead around Chinese New Year

📖 Fortune journal
• Keep a history of every question and reading
• Write private notes, and come back later to mark "Did it come true?"

✨ Share and personalize
• Share a beautiful scroll-style reading card with your friends
• Pick the color theme you like
• Use the app in English or Thai

💎 Premium
• 5 readings a day with no ads
• Ask the hermit follow-up questions about the same hexagram
• Listen to your reading read aloud, and keep an unlimited fortune journal
• 7-day free trial on the yearly plan

Start for free with one welcome AI reading, then watch a short ad to earn more readings. Casting the coins and reading the scripture text are free and unlimited.

For anyone curious about the I Ching, the Book of Changes, Chinese divination, oracle readings or fortune sticks, or anyone who wants a quiet moment to reflect before making a decision.

Note: Readings are generated by AI (Google Gemini) for entertainment and reflection. They are not medical, legal or financial advice.
