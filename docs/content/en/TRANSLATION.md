# English translation of the 64 hexagrams

## Principles

- **Own wording.** All English is our own translation. The classical text (judgments, images, line statements) was rendered from the classical Chinese (public domain), using the team's Thai version as the guide to interpretation. Descriptions, daily messages and year messages were translated from the Thai.
- **No copying.** Nothing is copied or closely paraphrased from copyrighted translations (Wilhelm/Baynes, Legge revisions and others). The hexagram names are coined from the meaning of each Chinese character; the Wilhelm set of names is not used.
- **Unified terms.** These terms are used the same way in every judgment, image and line:
  - 亨 is "flourishing"
  - 吉 is "good fortune"
  - 凶 is "misfortune"
  - 无咎 is "no fault"
- **Plain modern English.** Short, calm, polite sentences. No Thai words or Thai script; no religious terms specific to Thailand.
- **No meaning beyond the Thai.** Nothing is added, removed or made stronger than the Thai text. There is no investment, lending or medical advice, and no guaranteed outcomes.
- **Limits.** Daily message at most 120 characters; year message overview at most 200, work/love/money at most 80, blessing at most 60.
- **Format.** `hexagrams.en.json` has `version`, `trigrams` (the eight trigram patterns with `natureEn`) and `hexagrams` (the 64 overlay entries). The same file is bundled in the app and published on Hosting at `/content/hexagrams.en.json`.
