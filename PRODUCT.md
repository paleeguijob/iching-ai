# Product

## Platform

android

Compose Multiplatform app. Google Play ships first; iOS follows from the same shared UI once the Apple Developer account exists (docs/RELEASE.md section 9).

## Users

Thai people who enjoy fortune telling (สายมู) and already know practices like เซียมซี, but who mostly do not know the I Ching. Words like "กวา", "เส้นแปร" or "ตรีกราม" may mean nothing to them. They come with something on their mind and want an answer fast.

## Product Purpose

A Thai I Ching app: the user asks a question, casts six lines with three coins, and reads both the scripture and a Taoist-sage interpretation generated by AI. Siam Si (เซียมซี) is a second way to ask. A daily hexagram and a yearly hexagram bring people back. Success means people ask, return daily, and some convert to Premium (spec target ≥ 2%).

## Positioning

The interpretation rests on the real scripture (RAG over the 64 hexagrams and 384 lines), not random text, and it speaks in the voice of a Taoist sage. Library and Journal also set it apart from generic horoscope apps.

## Operating Context

- Home's main job is asking a question and casting. The daily hexagram comes second.
- Asking with an empty question is allowed (praying silently, อธิษฐานในใจ).
- Free users get one welcome reading for life, then readings from ads (up to 3 a day) or coins. Premium gets 5 readings a day.
- Casting and the scripture never use the quota. Only the AI guidance does.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home must show the remaining guidance (today's allowance, coins, ads left) before the user casts, following the quota table in docs/specs/2026-10-31-store-release-product-spec.md. The cast button works in every quota state.
- No watch-ad button on home. Ads are watched from the reading page.
- Questions are capped at 300 characters. Question categories are การงาน, ความรัก, การเงิน and สุขภาพใจ.
- The I Ching / Siam Si switch is shown only when Siam Si is enabled.
- The user confirmed that the streak week row and the yearly-hexagram line can move off home or shrink.
- Thai numerals are used for counts across home.
- Five user themes: Ink Paper, Bamboo, Cinnabar Dawn, Ink Night, Cinnabar Gold.

## Brand Commitments

- App name อี้จิง with a cinnabar seal 易.
- Persona: a Taoist sage (นักพรต) who speaks calmly and formally to "ท่าน".
- AI-generated content must be labelled as such, and the app carries the entertainment / not-advice disclaimer.

## Evidence on Hand

Product spec in docs/specs. Hexagram texts and daily messages are already written. There are no testimonials or usage numbers, so none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Asking comes first. Everything else on home must earn its place.
2. Plain Thai over jargon. Explain a term the first time a newcomer meets it.
3. Show the quota honestly. Never hide a cost or imply that casting is blocked.
4. Calm, not salesy. Upsell lives on the reading page, not on home.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Thai script needs generous line height. Text must not clip at font scale 1.3 or on narrow phones (spec acceptance criterion). Touch targets are at least 48dp.
